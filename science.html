Stage 1: Audio Capture

Technology: High-fidelity microphone input processing at 16kHz sampling rate.
Science: Captures acoustic waveforms with sufficient resolution to preserve voice characteristics including pitch, timbre, and formant frequencies critical for voice identity.

Stage 2: Speech-to-Text (STT)

Technology: Advanced automatic speech recognition (ASR) using neural acoustic models.
Science: Converts acoustic signals into phonetic representations, then into text using Hidden Markov Models and deep learning. Accounts for speech variations and disfluencies.

Stage 3: Linguistic Correction

Technology: Natural language processing with grammar correction and error detection.
Science: Applies computational linguistics to identify and correct grammatical errors, pronunciation mistakes, and semantic inconsistencies while preserving intended meaning.

Stage 4: Voice Synthesis

Technology: Neural Text-to-Speech (TTS) using Coqui TTS with your cloned voice model.
Science: Generates speech waveforms from text using deep neural networks trained on your voice sample. Preserves vocal characteristics: pitch range, speaking rate, and voice quality.

Stage 5: Prosody Transfer

Technology: Advanced prosodic feature extraction and transfer algorithms.
Science: Captures and transfers suprasegmental features (rhythm, stress, intonation) from your original speech to the corrected output, maintaining natural expression and emotional content.

Stage 6: Audio Playback

Technology: Low-latency audio rendering through optimized Rust audio bridge.
Science: Delivers corrected speech with <100ms latency, within the threshold for natural conversation (150ms), enabling real-time therapeutic feedback without disrupting communication flow.

🧠 Neurodiversity

Autism-Aware Voice Processing

Specialized algorithms designed to respect and support autistic communication patterns.

🎯 Extended Pause Tolerance

Research shows autistic individuals often use longer processing pauses (1-2 seconds) during speech. Goeckoh's Voice Activity Detection (VAD) system extends tolerance to 1.2 seconds, preventing premature cutoffs and respecting natural speech rhythms. This accommodates executive function differences and processing time needs without rushing the speaker.

🔊 Adjusted Sensitivity Thresholds

Autistic speech patterns may include quieter volume and reduced prosodic variation (monotone speech). Our VAD uses a lower threshold (0.45 vs standard 0.6) to reliably detect speech while filtering background noise, ensuring consistent recognition regardless of speaking volume or prosodic style.

💎 Sensory Overwhelm Monitoring

The Crystalline Heart system continuously monitors physiological and behavioral indicators of sensory overwhelm. When stress markers increase, the system automatically adjusts processing parameters, reduces feedback intensity, and provides calming interventions based on Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) principles.

💎 Emotional AI

The Crystalline Heart System

A 1024-node emotional regulation lattice that monitors, understands, and supports your emotional state.

🌐 Lattice Architecture

  • 1024 interconnected emotional nodes forming a coherent field
  • 8-dimensional emotional state space: joy, fear, anger, sadness, trust, disgust, anticipation, surprise
  • Real-time Global Coherence Level (GCL) computation measuring emotional stability
  • Adaptive co-regulation responding to detected emotional patterns
  • Crisis detection with automatic intervention protocols

🧬 How It Works

The Crystalline Heart uses a computational model inspired by neurobiological emotional processing. Each node represents an emotional micros state, with connections representing emotional relationships. Voice acoustic features (pitch variation, energy, speaking rate) are mapped to emotional dimensions. The system computes coherence across the lattice to detect emotional dysregulation, then applies therapeutic interventions including breathing exercises, positive affirmations, and calming audio cues.

📚 Evidence Base

Research Foundation

Goeckoh is built on decades of research in speech therapy, neuroscience, and autism support.

Voice Therapy Research

Studies show that hearing corrected speech in one's own voice (vs. synthetic voice) significantly improves learning outcomes and builds confidence. Self-voice feedback creates stronger neural associations and reduces anxiety associated with speech correction.

Autism Speech Patterns

Research on autistic communication identifies specific prosodic patterns, extended processing times, and sensory sensitivities. Goeckoh's design directly addresses these findings with customized VAD, reduced sensory load, and respect for individual processing speeds.

Real-Time Feedback

Neuroscience research demonstrates that immediate feedback (<150ms) is critical for motor learning and speech improvement. Delayed feedback can disrupt learning. Goeckoh's <100ms latency ensures optimal therapeutic effectiveness.

Emotional Regulation

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) and emotional regulation research supports the use of real-time monitoring, co-regulation strategies, and positive reinforcement. The Crystalline Heart implements these evidence-based approaches in a computational framework.
