The Self-Congruence Mandate

A radical departure from traditional AGI. Goeckoh is not a chatbot; it is a Corollary Discharge Proxy.

👤 The Architect

Designed by Jacob Graham, a 36-year-old self-taught engineer and father to a child on the autism spectrum. Operating outside institutional frameworks, Graham prioritized neurobiological fidelity over commercial metrics.

His goal: To disrupt the "black box" corporate AI model and deliver a privacy-first, 100% offline system that restores power and agency to the average person.

🎯 The Core Problem

Auditory Prediction Error (APE): In many neurodevelopmental conditions (ASD, TBI), the brain fails to predict the sound of its own voice. This creates "noise" instead of "self," inhibiting inner speech.

The Goeckoh Solution:

An external loop that captures intent, corrects it to first-person ("I want..."), and plays it back in a cloned voice within <150ms.

Therapeutic & Societal Impact

By operating 100% offline and prioritizing "Voice Agency," Goeckoh outperforms traditional digital assistants and standard therapy tools in critical dimensions for ASD, TBI, and Alzheimer's care.

🧩 Autism Spectrum (ASD)

Impact: Reduces cognitive overhead during communication. The cloned, corrected voice provides the consistent acoustic feedback required to scaffold inner dialogue.

🧠 Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)

Impact: The GCL Gate serves as an "external frontal lobe," preventing the AI from overwhelming the user during emotional lability while providing executive task breakdown.

👴 Alzheimer's & Dementia

Impact: Provides a "Voice of Continuity." The offline nature anchors the user in conversation without data privacy risks, preserving dignity through memory loss.
