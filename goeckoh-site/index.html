The Science

Your brain already knows how to fix this. It just needs the right signal.

Goeckoh is built on a principle called the Self-Correction Hypothesis — the idea that the brain can update its speech motor patterns when it hears its own voice corrected in real time.

The brain expects to hear your voice

Every time you speak, your motor cortex sends an advance prediction to your auditory cortex — a corollary discharge — of what your voice should sound like. This happens before you finish a word.1

The comparison window is ~200 milliseconds

Your brain compares its prediction to what it actually hears within roughly 200ms. Deliver the corrected voice inside that window, and the brain accepts it as self-produced — and can update accordingly.2

Your voice — not a replacement

Goeckoh corrects specific acoustic properties of your voice while leaving your identity, pitch, and emotional tone intact. It is not a synthetic voice. It is your voice, shaped.3
