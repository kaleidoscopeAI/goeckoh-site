❓ Help & Documentation

Getting Started

  1. Click "Start Visualization" and allow microphone access
  2. Speak or make sounds to see real-time analysis
  3. Use toggles to show/hide different visualizations
  4. Adjust gain and smoothing sliders for better results

Voice Metrics Explained

  • Pitch (Hz): Fundamental frequency - higher for females/children, lower for males
  • Zero Crossing Rate: How often signal crosses zero - indicates voicing vs noise
  • Spectral Centroid: "Center of mass" of frequencies - brightness of sound
  • Spectral Rolloff: Frequency below which 85% of energy is contained
  • Spectral Spread: Standard deviation of frequency distribution
  • Spectral Flatness: 0=tonal (voice), 1=noisy - indicates voice quality
  • Jitter: Pitch variation between frames - low is better for voice health
  • Shimmer: Amplitude variation - low indicates stable voice
  • HNR (Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio): Higher = clearer voice (>10dB is good)
  • Spectral Flux: Rate of spectral change - detects onsets and transitions

Voice Quality Score

Composite score (0-100) based on:

  • Low jitter and shimmer (voice stability)
  • High HNR (clarity)
  • Appropriate spectral flatness (tonal quality)
  • Consistent pitch

Voice Health Indicators

  • Normal: All metrics within healthy ranges
  • High Jitter/Shimmer: May indicate vocal strain or fatigue
  • Low HNR: Breathy or noisy voice - may need vocal rest
  • Unusual Pitch: Very high or low sustained pitch

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space - Start/Stop visualization
  • P - Pause/Resume
  • R - Toggle recording
  • S - Save snapshot
  • E - Export data
  • H - Show help

Tips for Best Results

  • Use a good quality microphone for accurate measurements
  • Maintain consistent distance from microphone
  • Minimize background noise
  • Adjust gain if signal is too weak or clipping
  • Higher FFT size = better frequency resolution but slower
  • Use comparison mode to track changes over time
