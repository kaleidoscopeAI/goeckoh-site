01 · A Normal Day

What a normal day actually looks like

No clinic visits, no scheduled sessions — it works whenever your child is speaking.

Morning
Earbuds go in like any other day
Any Bluetooth earbuds with a mic — the ones already in the house work. Open Goeckoh in the browser or the installed app, tap the correction profile that matches your child, and the phone goes in a pocket or bag. That's the entire startup routine.
Throughout the day
Correction happens during real conversation — not a drill
At school, at the grocery store, at the dinner table — anywhere your child speaks with the earbuds in, the correction is running. There's no "session" to schedule or complete. It's present during the actual moments understanding matters, not a 30-minute block set aside for practice.
Any time you want
You check in from your own device
Open the guardian view on your own phone or laptop, enter the session code, and see live acoustic data from your child's session in real time — from a separate room, a separate building, or across town. See "How guardian monitoring really works," below, for exactly what that connection is and isn't.
End of day
Session history stays on the device that made it
Structured progress data — phonological targets, fluency events — is stored locally in the browser on your child's own device. There's no account dashboard on our servers accumulating a history of your child's voice; the record lives where the session happened.
02 · Setup

Getting started — the actual steps

From signing up to your child's first corrected conversation.

1
Start early access and get a license key
Sign up on the early access page. Your license key arrives by email immediately — it's what unlocks the app on your child's device and any additional devices under your plan.
2
Open Goeckoh on your child's device
It runs directly in Chrome or Safari — no install required — or as a native app on desktop, Windows, Linux, or Android if you prefer one. Enter the license key once; it stays activated on that device.
3
Pick a correction profile
Choose the profile that matches what you're addressing — ASD-related speech patterns, dysarthria, apraxia, or post-stroke/TBI speech changes. This calibrates which vowel targets the correction engine uses; you can change it any time.
4
Connect your own device as a guardian monitor
On your child's device, generate a session code. Enter that same code on your own phone or laptop's guardian view. You're now connected for that session — see below for exactly what that connection carries.
5
Put the earbuds on and go about the day
That's it. No calibration recording, no waiting period. Correction begins the moment your child starts speaking with the earbuds in.
03 · Guardian Monitoring

How guardian monitoring really works

The specific mechanism — not a vague "you can check in" promise.

Each session, your child's device generates a one-time session code — a short string of characters, unique to that session. Enter that same code on your own device's guardian view, and the two devices open a direct, live connection to each other for as long as that session runs.

What travels over that connection is structured metric data only — the same kind of acoustic readout shown on the child's own screen: formant positions, voice quality measures, fluency events. It is never raw audio. Your child's voice itself never leaves their device, to your device or anywhere else — the correction engine and microphone stay local to the device that's actually being used; only the derived numbers describing what's happening get relayed to you, live.

The relay itself does not keep a copy of anything that passes through it. It exists only to forward data from your child's active session to whichever guardian devices are currently connected with the matching code — the moment the session ends, or either device disconnects, there's nothing left on our servers from that connection. If you close the guardian view and reopen it later, you're not resuming a stored history from the server; each session's live view exists only while that session is actually running.

What you'll actually see: the same real-time formant, voice-quality, and fluency readout your child's own device shows, updating live as they speak. What you won't see or hear: their raw voice audio, a recording, or a stored history on our end — session history that persists across time lives only in local storage on your child's own device, the same as described in the On-Device Architecture section of the Science page.
04 · Comfort & Safety

Comfort, safety, and the questions parents actually ask

Answered directly, not deflected into a generic FAQ.

Does my child have to wear earbuds all day?

No. Correction only happens while the earbuds are in and the app is active. There's no requirement to keep them in for a set number of hours — many families start with short, comfortable stretches and extend from there as the child adjusts to having something in their ear.

Is the volume safe for a child's ears?

The corrected signal plays back at a volume you control, comparable to a normal phone call through earbuds — not amplified beyond a comfortable listening level. There's no electrical contact with the ear or body anywhere in the design; it's audio in, audio out.

Will my child still sound like themselves?

Yes. The correction shapes formant structure only — pitch, speaking rate, and overall voice quality are untouched. It's not a synthetic voice and it's not a replacement voice; see "What Formant Correction Actually Changes" on the Science page for the exact acoustic line being drawn.

What if they resist wearing earbuds at first?

That's common, especially for children with sensory sensitivities. There's no harm in introducing them gradually — a few minutes at a time, during a preferred activity — since the correction only needs to be present during the moments you choose to use it, not continuously.

Does this replace speech therapy?

No — it's built to supplement it. Many families use it alongside an existing speech-language pathologist, and clinicians can review the same structured session data (see the For Clinicians page). It's designed to extend the moments a therapy session can't reach, not to replace clinical care.

What happens if we stop using it?

Nothing is lost or locked away — session history stays in your child's own browser storage regardless of subscription status, and there's no dependency created; Goeckoh corrects feedback in the moment, it doesn't replace any skill your child already has.

05 · Realistic Expectations

What's realistic to expect

Said plainly, because a health product that oversells itself isn't one you should trust.

Goeckoh gives the brain a more accurate, better-timed version of its own auditory feedback — that's the entire mechanism, laid out in full on the Science and Research pages. What that produces, in practice, varies by person: some families notice a difference in how often they need to ask their child to repeat themselves within the first weeks; for others, especially where speech differences are longstanding or severe, change is slower and more incremental. Speech motor learning is real but it isn't instant, and no single tool — corrected feedback included — is a substitute for the individualized judgment of a speech-language pathologist who knows your child.

What we can say with confidence is the mechanism itself: real-time, corrected auditory feedback delivered inside the biological window where the brain can use it is a legitimate, peer-reviewed basis for motor learning. What we won't say is a specific timeline or outcome for your child, because no honest product can promise that in advance.
