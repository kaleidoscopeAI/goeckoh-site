💝 About Us

Our Mission & Story

Building compassionate AI technology to support neurodivergent individuals and anyone seeking communication enhancement.

The Goeckoh Story

Goeckoh (pronounced "Go Echo") was born from a simple yet profound observation: traditional speech therapy tools often use synthetic, unfamiliar voices that can create anxiety and reduce therapeutic effectiveness, especially for neurodivergent individuals.

We asked ourselves: what if therapy could use your own voice? What if technology could be gentle, respectful, and truly understanding of neurodivergent communication patterns? What if we could combine cutting-edge AI with deep compassion for individual differences?

The answer is Goeckoh - a neural voice system that listens without judgment, corrects with kindness, and supports with understanding. Every feature is designed with autism awareness, from extended pause tolerance to sensory overwhelm monitoring.

We believe that communication support should be accessible, private, and respectful. That's why Goeckoh runs entirely on your device, never sending your voice data anywhere. That's why it's open source, allowing anyone to verify, modify, and improve the system.

Our Mission

To empower individuals with communication challenges through compassionate, intelligent, and privacy-respecting technology that celebrates neurodiversity and supports personal growth.
