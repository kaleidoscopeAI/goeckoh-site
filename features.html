🎯 Core Features

The Foundation

🎤

Neural Voice Cloning

Real-Time Privacy-First High-Fidelity

Advanced neural text-to-speech technology that captures and preserves your unique voice characteristics. Your voice is cloned locally on your device and used to provide corrected speech feedback in your own voice, not a synthetic one.

Key Capabilities:

  • Requires only 5+ seconds of voice sample for cloning
  • Preserves pitch, timbre, speaking rate, and prosodic patterns
  • Adapts to your voice over time with continual learning
  • Maintains emotional expression and natural variation
  • 100% local processing - your voice never leaves your device
⚡

Real-Time Processing

<100ms Latency Rust-Powered Optimized

Ultra-low latency voice processing enables natural conversation flow without disruption. Powered by a high-performance Rust audio core that processes your voice faster than you can notice.

Processing Pipeline:

  • Audio capture at 16kHz with high-fidelity preservation
  • Instant speech-to-text conversion using optimized ASR
  • Real-time grammatical and pronunciation correction
  • Neural voice synthesis in your own voice
  • Prosody transfer maintaining natural expression
  • Audio playback with <100ms total latency
🧠

Autism-Optimized VAD

Extended Pauses Lower Threshold Respectful

Voice Activity Detection specifically designed for autistic communication patterns. Respects processing pauses, quiet speech, and reduced prosodic variation without interruption.

Specialized Adaptations:

  • 1.2 second pause tolerance (vs. standard 0.5s) for processing time
  • Lower sensitivity threshold (0.45) for quiet or monotone speech
  • Filters background noise while preserving speech
  • Respects natural speech rhythms without rushing
  • Configurable parameters for individual needs
💎

Crystalline Heart Emotional System

1024 Nodes 8D States Real-Time

Advanced emotional regulation lattice that monitors and supports your emotional state in real-time. Detects stress, provides co-regulation, and offers therapeutic interventions when needed.

Emotional Intelligence:

  • 1024 interconnected emotional nodes forming coherent field
  • 8D emotional state tracking: joy, fear, anger, sadness, trust, disgust, anticipation, surprise
  • Global Coherence Level (GCL) computation for stability monitoring
  • Real-time crisis detection with automatic interventions
  • Sensory overwhelm detection and calming protocols
  • ABA-based therapeutic interventions and positive reinforcement
🌌

3D Cognitive Nebula Visualization

Interactive Beautiful Engaging

Stunning 3D visualization of your voice and emotional state using React and Three.js. Transforms therapy into an engaging, visual experience with real-time feedback.

Visual Features:

  • Real-time voice bubble visualization showing speech patterns
  • AI-generated images from voice input creating unique visual feedback
  • Interactive 3D universe that responds to your voice
  • Emotional state visualization with color and movement
  • Therapeutic gaming mechanics for engagement
  • Customizable visual themes and preferences
